What this model does not include
μr = 1 everywhere — no iron. With no yoke to carry the return flux, even the line hugging the bore wall closes out at r ≈ 15; a real stator would bring it back through the back-iron in a few centimetres. No saturation either. Magnetoquasistatic: E shown is −∂A/∂t only, the part that does the work; conductor surface charge is dropped. Rotor is drawn but carries no current, so there is no slip, no induced rotor field and no torque.
