Pull the phases apart: more field occupies the space between them, so flux linkage and L rise. Make the conductor thicker: the internal cutoff moves outward, so L falls. Sag changes the picture, but this readout uses the average horizontal geometry.
Reading the field
Every arrow is the summed B vector from all three currents. The side projection samples Bz in the longitudinal plane through the centre conductor: warm points emerge from the screen, violet points enter it, and brightness is magnitude. Points are samples, not particles or trajectories. Along an electrically long line the model applies the local TEM current phase to each cross-section; the displayed length is compressed. Because iA+iB+iC=0, the field fades rapidly far from the line. The familiar formula is L = 2×10⁻⁷ ln(Deq/GMR) H/m.
