Pull the phases apart: more field occupies the space between them, so flux linkage and L rise. Make the conductor thicker: the internal cutoff moves outward, so L falls. Sag changes the picture, but this readout uses the average horizontal geometry.
Reading the field
Arrow direction follows the right-hand rule. Brightness and length represent |B|. Between conductors, fields may reinforce or cancel as the three currents exchange roles; because iA+iB+iC=0, the field fades rapidly far from the line. The familiar formula is L = 2×10⁻⁷ ln(Deq/GMR) H/m.
