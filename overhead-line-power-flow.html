Reading the model
E comes from conductor charge solved from the phase-to-ground voltages. H comes from conductor current; B = μ₀H. Their physical-space cross product gives local power density. Integrating over the cross-section recovers the circuit-state product Σvᵢiᵢ. Individual phase powers pulsate, while balanced total P is constant.

Voltage and current axes in the small plot are state-space axes, not spatial directions. The line is electrically short: no propagation delay or reflections. Ground is idealized by image charges and currents; losses, corona and terminal fringing fields are omitted.
