Reading the model
Each edge of the box measures one quantity: potential in volts, flow in amperes, time in seconds, each to its own scale. Each face is the product of its two edges: potential × flow is power (W), flow × time is charge (C), potential × time is volt-seconds (V·s). The volume is energy (J). With the unit grid on, every cell on a face is one unit of that product.

These are state-space axes, not directions in space: nothing here is a field.
